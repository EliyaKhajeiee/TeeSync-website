3. What TeeSync Does

TeeSync helps groups find, coordinate, and book golf tee times through iMessage. A host chooses a course, date, and time, sends a TeeSync invitation to a group, collects accept/decline responses, and books the round on the group's behalf.

TeeSync is a booking and coordination platform, not a golf course operator. Tee time availability, pricing, course conditions, dress codes, pace-of-play rules, and on-site policies are set and controlled by each golf course, not by us. We are not responsible for the acts or omissions of any golf course.

Course availability, features, and geographic coverage may change. We may add, modify, suspend, or discontinue any part of the Service at any time.

5. Bookings and Pricing

  • Course-direct pricing. All tee time prices displayed in TeeSync are set by the golf course. TeeSync does not mark up tee times and does not add service fees, convenience fees, or booking fees.
  • The host books for the group. When a host confirms a booking, the host is placing a reservation for every player in the group and is responsible for it, including any charges the course applies for the full party.
  • Accepting an invite is a commitment. If you accept a TeeSync invitation, you're agreeing to show up or to notify the host in time for them to make changes under the course's policy.
  • Confirmation. A booking is confirmed only when TeeSync displays a confirmation and the course accepts the reservation. Occasionally a tee time may become unavailable between selection and confirmation; if that happens, we'll tell you and you won't be charged for that reservation.
  • Accuracy. We work to keep availability and pricing accurate, but information comes from third-party course systems and may contain errors or lag. We reserve the right to correct errors and to cancel a booking made at an obviously incorrect price, with a full refund of anything charged.

6. Payments

Depending on the course, payment is collected either through TeeSync's third-party payment processor at the time of booking, or directly by the course at check-in. TeeSync itself is free to download and use.

Where payment is collected through the app, you authorize us and our processor to charge your selected payment method for the amount shown at checkout, plus any taxes the course applies. You represent that you're authorized to use that payment method. We do not store full card numbers; payments are handled by a PCI-compliant processor.

Where the group splits costs, TeeSync may send payment reminders as a convenience. Settling up within your group is between you and your group — TeeSync does not guarantee, collect, or enforce payments between players.

7. Cancellations, Changes, and Refunds

Cancellation and refund policies are set by each golf course, not by TeeSync. Some courses allow free cancellation up to a stated cutoff; others charge a fee or are non-refundable. The applicable policy is shown before you confirm a booking — please read it.

  • To cancel or change a booking, use the TeeSync app where supported, or contact the course directly.
  • Where a refund is due under the course's policy, we'll process it back to the original payment method. Depending on your bank, refunds typically take 5–10 business days to appear.
  • No-shows and late cancellations may be charged in full at the course's discretion.
  • Weather, course closures, and maintenance are handled under the course's policy. We'll help you communicate with the course, but we can't override its decisions.
  • If TeeSync makes a booking error that is our fault, contact us at info@tee-sync.com and we'll work with the course to make it right.
