Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about TeeSync and how the platform works. Still have a question? Reach out.

TeeSync is a golf app that makes it effortless to coordinate and book tee times with a group. Instead of juggling multiple websites, screenshots in a group chat, and endless back-and-forth, TeeSync handles everything in one place — from finding availability and polling your crew, to booking and sending reminders — all inside iMessage.

Only the host does. TeeSync is a free iPhone app — download it from the App Store — and the person organizing the round needs it installed. Once the host sends a TeeSync invitation through iMessage, everyone else in the group can accept or decline right in the chat without installing anything. The host then books the tee time for the whole group based on who's in.

The host picks a course, date, and tee time, then sends a TeeSync invitation to the group via iMessage. Each person accepts or declines — that's it. Once responses are in, the host confirms and TeeSync books the tee time for everyone. No coordinating across multiple apps, no one left out of the loop.

Yes. TeeSync handles all the follow-up automatically. Once a tee time is booked, everyone gets a confirmation. Leading up to the round, TeeSync sends reminders so nobody forgets — including a weather update the morning of. Payment reminders are also sent so the group stays on top of any outstanding costs.

No. TeeSync is committed to transparent, course-direct pricing. All tee time prices are set directly by the golf course — what you see on TeeSync is exactly what you pay. There are no hidden fees, no service charges, and no surprise markups at checkout.

TeeSync is built for iPhone and lives inside iMessage. You'll need an iPhone running iOS to use the app. All group members who receive a TeeSync invitation can accept or decline directly through iMessage — no extra setup required on their end beyond having an iPhone.

The app is free to download and free to use. You only pay for the round itself, at the price the course sets — no booking fees, no service charges, no subscription, and no markup from us.

Cancellation and refund policies are set by each golf course, not by TeeSync — the applicable policy is shown before the host confirms a booking. Where a course allows it, the host can cancel or change the round in the app; otherwise contact the course directly. If a refund is owed, it goes back to the original payment method, typically within 5–10 business days. Full details are in our Terms & Conditions.
